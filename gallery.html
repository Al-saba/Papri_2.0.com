Skill Development

PAPRI Skill Development Centre: Building Livelihoods

Our dedicated technical training hub equips marginalized youth, women and smallholder families with practical, market-oriented skills in tailoring, handicrafts, computer literacy and small business management.

Adult Literacy

Mayer School: Educating Rural Mothers

Our flagship adult education initiative helps rural mothers who missed formal schooling gain functional literacy, numeracy and household financial management skills, alongside maternal and child health awareness.

Get in Touch to Learn More
Youth Leadership

PAPRI Youth Change-Makers in Action

Our youth wing leads volunteerism, civic awareness and environmental action across Chuadanga, tackling issues like child marriage, acid violence and child labor while building the next generation of local leaders.

Agriculture

Farmer Field Schools: Learning by Doing

PAPRI

People Advancement Power Rural Institute (PAPRI) is a non-governmental development organization working since 2003 to build a resilient, self-reliant, and equitable rural society across Chuadanga, Bangladesh.
