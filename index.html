About PAPRI

A Grassroots Organization Working at the Intersection of Education, Livelihoods and Climate Resilience to Transform Rural Bangladesh.

2003
Founding
Year

Founded in 2003 in Chuadanga, PAPRI is a registered non-governmental development organization built on grassroots empowerment and social equity. We work directly inside rural communities to expand access to education, income, and healthcare.

From adult literacy classrooms to farmer field schools and youth-led climate action, every program is designed to be participatory, sustainable and community-owned.

What We Do

Six Core Program Areas Designed to Build Lasting, Community-Owned Change

01

Youth Development & Education

We equip rural youth with digital literacy, life skills and leadership training so they can lead change in their own communities.

  • Digital & Skills Training
  • Leadership & Life Skills
  • Youth-Led Networking Platforms
02

Climate Resilience & Environment

From tree plantation drives to waste management, we help communities adapt to a changing climate and protect their local ecosystems.

  • Tree Plantation Drives
  • Climate Awareness Campaigns
  • Eco-Friendly Waste Management
03

Agriculture & Rural Development

We introduce sustainable farming methods and run Farmer Field Schools so smallholder families can grow more with fewer resources.

  • Sustainable Farming Practices
  • Farmer Training & Capacity Building
  • Community Farmer Field Schools
04

Healthcare & Sanitation

We run health camps, mobile clinics and WASH programs that bring safe water, sanitation and basic care to underserved villages.

  • Health Awareness Campaigns
  • WASH & Hygiene Programs
  • Health Camps & Mobile Clinics
05

Women Empowerment

Through livelihood training and self-help groups, we help women build independent income and greater voice in household decisions.

  • Livelihood & Skills Training
  • Legal Rights Awareness
  • Self-Help Group Formation
06

Research & Development

Knowledge-based development guiding every program we run, from needs assessments to impact evaluation.

  • Needs Assessments & Evaluations
  • Academic & Government Partnerships
  • Community-Participatory Methods
