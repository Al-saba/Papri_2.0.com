About PAPRI

A Grassroots Organization Working at the Intersection of Education, Livelihoods and Climate Resilience to Transform Rural Bangladesh.

2003
Founding
Year

Founded in 2003 in Chuadanga, PAPRI is a registered non-governmental development organization built on grassroots empowerment and social equity. We work directly inside rural communities to expand access to education, income, and healthcare.

From adult literacy classrooms to farmer field schools and youth-led climate action, every program is designed to be participatory, sustainable and community-owned.

What We Do

Six Core Program Areas Designed to Build Lasting, Community-Owned Change

01

Youth Development & Education

We equip rural youth with digital literacy, life skills and leadership training so they can lead change in their own communities.

  • Digital & Skills Training
  • Leadership & Life Skills
  • Youth-Led Networking Platforms
02

Climate Resilience & Environment

From tree plantation drives to waste management, we help communities adapt to a changing climate and protect their local ecosystems.

  • Tree Plantation Drives
  • Climate Awareness Campaigns
  • Eco-Friendly Waste Management
03

Agriculture & Rural Development

We introduce sustainable farming methods and run Farmer Field Schools so smallholder families can grow more with fewer resources.

  • Sustainable Farming Practices
  • Farmer Training & Capacity Building
  • Community Farmer Field Schools
04

Healthcare & Sanitation

We run health camps, mobile clinics and WASH programs that bring safe water, sanitation and basic care to underserved villages.

  • Health Awareness Campaigns
  • WASH & Hygiene Programs
  • Health Camps & Mobile Clinics
05

Women Empowerment

Through livelihood training and self-help groups, we help women build independent income and greater voice in household decisions.

  • Livelihood & Skills Training
  • Legal Rights Awareness
  • Self-Help Group Formation
Our Approach

How We Turn Community Needs Into Lasting, Local Solutions

01

Community Needs Assessment

We work directly with village leaders and households to identify real priorities, from literacy gaps to farming and health challenges.
02

Program Design

Our team designs participatory programs — training curricula, farmer schools, health camps — tailored to each community\'s context.
03

Grassroots Delivery

Field staff and trained volunteers deliver programs directly in villages, ensuring every session is accessible, practical and locally relevant.
04

Monitoring & Sustainability

We track outcomes through ongoing research and evaluation, and hand over ownership to community groups for long-term sustainability.

Ongoing Initiatives

Grassroots Projects Creating Measurable Change, One Village at a Time

Climate Resilience

নব জীবন সবুজাযন (New Life Greenery)

Commemorating the birth of every newborn with a tree planting. Over 500 newborns and their families have taken part so far, growing an equal number of trees across local communities.

View Case Study
Nutrition & Agriculture

আমার পুষ্টি বাগান (Aamar Pushti Bagan)

Encouraging households to grow their own nutritious vegetables and fruit. More than 100 households, benefiting roughly 4,000 people, now run their own nutrition gardens.

View Case Study
Women's Health

Women's Health in Cancer Prevention

Raising awareness on cervical and breast cancer, with early screening and prevention education. Reached over 5,000 women and screened more than 500 individuals.

View Case Study
Youth Development

Sports for Social Change

Using the power of play to build leadership, teamwork and discipline among rural youth. Over 1,200 young people have taken part, with improved confidence and social skills.
