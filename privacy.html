PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

People Advancement Power Rural Institute (PAPRI) respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website, including community members, donors, volunteers, and partners. This policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

We collect only the information that is necessary to respond to your inquiries, process volunteer applications, or communicate about our programs. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

What Information Do We Collect?

When you contact us through this website, subscribe to updates, or reach out about volunteering, donating, or partnering with PAPRI, we may collect basic information such as your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message. We may also collect general, non-identifying technical information (such as browser type or approximate location) to help us understand how visitors use this site and to improve it over time.

How Do We Use Your Information?

Information you share with us is used to respond to your inquiries, process volunteer or partnership requests, keep you informed about PAPRI's programs and news (if you have opted in), and improve our website and services. We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond what you would reasonably expect based on your interaction with us.

Photographs and Community Stories

PAPRI's programs work directly with community members across Chuadanga and surrounding districts. Photographs and stories featured on this website are shared to illustrate our work honestly and respectfully. If you are featured on this site and would like your photo or story removed or amended, please contact us and we will address your request promptly.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to our team at ngopapri@gmail.com or through our Contact page.

PAPRI

People Advancement Power Rural Institute (PAPRI) is a non-governmental development organization working since 2003 to build a resilient, self-reliant, and equitable rural society across Chuadanga, Bangladesh.
