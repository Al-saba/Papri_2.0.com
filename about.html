Organizational Profile

A Registered, Community-Rooted Development Organization Serving Rural Bangladesh Since 2003.

2003
Founding
Year

People Advancement Power Rural Institute (PAPRI) is a non-governmental, non-profit community development organization established in 2003 in Chuadanga, Bangladesh, founded on grassroots empowerment and social equity.

Vision: A resilient, self-reliant and equitable rural society where every individual has access to education, economic stability, healthy living and environmental security.

Mission: To empower rural communities through functional education, youth leadership, environmental stewardship and local health & social welfare initiatives.

Legal Status & Registration

A Legally Recognized Development Institution Built on Two Decades of Community Trust

01

Registered Institution

PAPRI is registered under the Ministry of Social Welfare, Bangladesh, recognized as a formal social development institution.

  • Ministry of Social Welfare
  • Recognized NGO Status
  • Operating Since 2003
02

Voluntary Social Welfare Ordinance, 1961

Registered under the Voluntary Social Welfare Agencies (Registration and Control) Ordinance, 1961, granting legal standing to operate.

  • Legally Recognized Agency
  • Government Oversight
  • Compliant Operations
03

Societies Registration Act, 1860

Also registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (amended 1962), further establishing formal legal identity.

  • Formal Society Status
  • Amended 1962
  • Full Legal Standing
04

Transparent Governance

A 12-member board of directors oversees finance, human resources, research, communications and program delivery.

  • 17-Member Board
  • Financial Accountability
  • Program Oversight
05

20+ Years of Operation

Two decades of continuous community service across Chuadanga and surrounding rural districts of southwestern Bangladesh.

  • Established 2003
  • Deep Community Trust
  • Proven Track Record
