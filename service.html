OUR SERVICE

What We Do

Seven Core Program Areas Driving Rural Transformation Across Southwestern Bangladesh

01

Youth Development & Education

Programs that build the skills, confidence and leadership rural youth need to shape their own future and their communities.

  • Skills Training & Digital Learning
  • Leadership & Life Skills
  • Youth Networking Platforms
02

Climate Resilience & Environment

Helping communities adapt to a changing climate through tree plantation, awareness and sustainable waste practices.

  • Tree Plantation Drives
  • Climate Change Awareness
  • Eco-Friendly Waste Management
03

Agriculture & Rural Development

Introducing modern, sustainable farming methods so smallholder families can grow more while protecting their land.

  • Sustainable Farming Practices
  • Farmer Training & Capacity Building
  • Farmer Field Schools
04

Healthcare & Sanitation

Bringing basic care, clean water and hygiene education directly into villages that lack easy access to health services.

  • Health Awareness Campaigns
  • WASH Programs & Hygiene
  • Health Camps & Mobile Clinics
05

Women Empowerment

Building women's independent income, legal awareness and collective voice through training and self-help groups.

  • Livelihood & Skill Training
  • Gender Equality & Legal Rights
  • Self-Help Group Formation
Research & Development

Knowledge-Based Development Guiding Every Program We Run

Needs Assessments & Evaluations
We run qualitative and quantitative research, including needs assessments, impact evaluations and case studies, before and after every program.
Academic & Government Partnerships
We collaborate with academic institutions, development partners and government agencies on joint field research and program design.
Community-Participatory Methods
Every study is grounded in direct community participation, ensuring findings reflect the real priorities of the people we serve.

PAPRI
