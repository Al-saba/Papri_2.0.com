NEWS & PROJECTS

Nabo Jibon Sobujayon: New Life Greenery

2026 Climate Resilience

Commemorating the birth of every newborn with a tree planting. Over 500 newborns and their families have taken part so far, growing an equal number of trees across local communities.

Get in Touch to Learn More

Aamar Pushti Bagan: Nutrition Gardens

2026 Nutrition & Agriculture

Encouraging households to grow their own nutritious vegetables and fruit. More than 100 households, benefiting roughly 4,000 people, now run their own nutrition gardens with five fruit trees each.

Get in Touch to Learn More

Women's Health in Cancer Prevention

2026 Women's Health

Raising awareness on cervical and breast cancer, with early screening and prevention education. Reached over 5,000 women through campaigns and screened more than 500 individuals.

Get in Touch to Learn More

Surokkhito Shoishob: Protecting Our Children

2026 Child Protection

Teaching children about good touch and bad touch. Workshops for over 3,000 children and 1,500 parents and guardians have fostered a safer, more aware environment.

Get in Touch to Learn More

Osomoye Bibaho Noy, Bhobishshoter Joy

2026 Women & Youth Rights

Raising awareness on the harms of child marriage through education, empowerment and legal aid. Our interventions have directly prevented over 200 child marriages and reached more than 4,000 community members.

Get in Touch to Learn More

Sports for Social Change

2026 Youth Development

Using the power of play to build leadership, teamwork, discipline and social values among rural youth. Over 1,200 young people have taken part, with improved confidence and social skills.

Get in Touch to Learn More
PAPRI
